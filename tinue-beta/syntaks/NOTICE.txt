Syntaks — Tak engine (6×6), a group project by Ciekce and contributors
https://github.com/Ciekce/syntaks (engine, MIT licence below)
https://github.com/Ciekce/syntaks-nets (cnet023 net, trained by chef; releases/cnet023)

syntaks.wasm in this directory is Syntaks at upstream commit 4f224c9 with tinue's single-threaded WebAssembly
patch (wasm/syntaks/syntaks-wasm.patch in the tinue repository), built by wasm/syntaks/build.sh. It embeds the
cnet023 network, redistributed with Ciekce's permission (2026-11-15).

It also includes the Rust crate arrayvec 0.7.6 (bluss and contributors; MIT OR Apache-2.0, used here under MIT,
text below) and the Rust standard library (MIT OR Apache-2.0). The atomic-wait stand-in in wasm/syntaks is tinue's.

worker.js (this directory) and ../syntaks.js are part of tinue, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later.
