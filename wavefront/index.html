its model of you · prediction accuracy

every quarter second it writes down your situation and what you did, and trains a small net to predict it. that net is who it fights in its dreams.

its dreams

dreamed play—
policy updates—
decisiveness—

it trains on 256 dreamed duels at a time, started from moments of your real game and played against its model of you, by reinforcement (ppo), never on your real game directly.

what you see: 32 futures from right now, each about 1.3 s, refreshed 7× a second—its policy against its model of you, the same way it dreams. cyan paths are you, violet are it. a coral ring is where it imagines hitting you; a cyan ring, where it imagines you hitting it. these are only drawn, never trained on. the button cycles to the training dreams themselves.

its incentives

small costs in its dreams: every tick it spends inside its personal space around you (growing as it gets closer), and every tick it's pressed against a wall. changes apply to its dreams immediately; its behaviour follows as it retrains (about half a minute). 0 lets it sit on you.
