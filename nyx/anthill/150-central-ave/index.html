Move-in feasibility: ✗ long shot
MAJOR RED FLAG: Description explicitly states 3 of 4 units are tenant-occupied with in-place income, only the oversized 3BR unit is vacant. For a group-house lease, you need the entire building vacant. SF tenant protections make evicting existing tenants extremely difficult—requires just cause (Ellis Act, owner move-in, etc.), 120+ days notice, relocation payments averaging $7k-$20k per tenant, and potential legal challenges. Even if buyer negotiates vacant possession at close, existing tenants have strong legal protections and the process typically takes 6-12+ months. The one vacant 3BR unit could theoretically house 3-4 people immediately, but that defeats the purpose of an 8-person group house. Unless the seller pre-negotiates buyouts with all tenants or uses Ellis Act (which requires taking the entire building off rental market for 5+ years), this is not realistically move-in ready for a whole-building group lease. Best case: 6-12 month timeline with significant legal/buyout costs. Worst case: tied up in litigation for years.
