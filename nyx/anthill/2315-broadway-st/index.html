Set along one of Pacific Heights' most prominent blocks, the Broadway Manor is a landmark 1897 Queen Anne Victorian and one of San Francisco's original great mansions. Offered for sale for the first time since 1969, this home has been expansively renovated and reimagined by some of Northern California's most celebrated designers as the 2026 SF Decorator Showcase. The result is a rare balance of historic integrity and elevated modern design. Sweeping North and West views of the GG Bridge open from each of the home's four levels. A grand formal foyer introduces the main level, where original millwork, the period staircase, and stained-glass windows have been carefully restored and set against custom finishes that bring the house into the present. Nine fireplaces are distributed throughout. The formal living and dining rooms are proportioned for both intimate evenings and large, celebratory gatherings. At the center of the home, the one-of-a-kind kitchen is anchored by a marble-clad island and detailed with bespoke cabinetry and a butler's pantry. The kitchen and parlor open directly to a remarkably private, south-facing yard, extending daily living and entertaining outdoors. The grand staircase continues up to the main bedroom level, where in the primary suite, barrel bay windows frame iconic views. The suite includes a dressing room and a striking bathroom with a soaking tub and walk-in shower. Three additional bedrooms, two bathrooms, sunroom, office, and a luxe laundry room. The top floor is a defining moment of the home, where the view reaches its fullest expression. This level unfolds to a media room with a wet bar, one en-suite bedroom, and another bedroom with a stunning view deck and a full bathroom. The ground level is a full-floor legal second unit with two bedrooms, two baths, living room, kitchen, laundry, and ample storage. The home has a two-car garage. Designed by Moses J. Lyon, whose work shaped many of the city's notable landmarks, the residence reflects a legacy of architectural significance. Spanning 9,325 square feet, this home has been the residence of the family of the renowned architect, legendary bon vivant, Herbert P. McLaughlin for over 50 years. Positioned at the intersection of Broadway and Fillmore, the home is moments from Alta Plaza Park, the Fillmore corridor, Union St, the Marina, the Presidio, and the GG Bridge. Several of the city's top private schools are within three blocks, placing convenience alongside prestige.
