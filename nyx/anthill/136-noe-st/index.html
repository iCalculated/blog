136-144 Noe Street is a nine (9) unit building located in the Duboce Triangle between Henry and 14th Streets. Constructed in 1900, the property balances Victorian charm with modern comforts and upgrades. Seven of the nine units have been extensively updated with new kitchens, appliances, bathrooms, and in-unit laundry. The peaceful, tree-lined block of Noe Street offers a respite from the hustle + bustle of the city with very light car traffic thanks to the slow street designation. The location offers tenants easy walking access to CPMC Davies Hospital (1 block), Duboce Park, Muni rail service on Duboce (N-Train) or Castro Station, and the shops/restaurants on Market Street. The generous layouts average over 1,000 sq/ft per unit. The unit mix (one 2bd-1ba, two 2bd-2ba, four 3bd-2ba, and two 4bd-2ba) is well-suited to accommodate roommates or families. One 2bd-1ba unit will be delivered vacant. Seven of the nine units have laundry in-unit. The property operates efficiently with separate meters for PGE, separate water heaters, and limited deferred maintenance.
